Privacy Policy

Remtoo collects only the information needed to provide the service: your name and email when you create an account, and usage data (lessons opened, progress completed) that powers your dashboard. We never sell personal data, and we share it only with the service providers that operate our infrastructure (hosting, email, payments) under strict data-processing agreements.

You can request a copy or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to privacy@remtoo.com. Deleting your account removes your personal data within 30 days, except where we are legally required to keep records (for example, invoices).

Terms of Service

By using Remtoo you agree to these terms. Subscriptions are month-to-month and can be cancelled at any time from your account; access continues until the end of the paid period. New subscriptions are covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee.

  • Remtoo grants you a license to use the materials with your own students, in your own classes.
  • You may not resell, redistribute, or publicly republish Remtoo content.
  • School Plan licenses cover up to 20 teachers at one location unless otherwise agreed.
  • We may update these terms; material changes will be announced by email in advance.

Questions about these terms? Contact hello@remtoo.com.

Cookie Policy

Remtoo uses a small number of strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and remember your preferences (such as the level you last browsed). We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Analytics, where enabled, is privacy-friendly and aggregated.

Because only strictly necessary cookies are used, no consent banner is required under the ePrivacy framework. If that ever changes, we will ask first.

GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Remtoo acts as the data controller for the personal data described in the Privacy Policy. Your rights of access, rectification, erasure, portability, and objection can be exercised at any time via privacy@remtoo.com, and you may lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

Data is stored on servers located within the EU or in countries with an adequacy decision, and transfers outside those areas rely on Standard Contractual Clauses.
